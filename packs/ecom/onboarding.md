# Onboarding – ecom (runs after the general onboarding)

Asks only for what the general round didn't cover. Output: appended to `brain/company.md`; brand material to `brain/brand/`.

## 1. Shop
- Which shop platform? Carrier? (→ connector setup)
- Who packs orders and when? How many days to deliver?
- Return / warranty rules I should know?

## 2. Brand
- Brandbook, logo, colours, old posts somewhere? (link/folder → `brain/brand/`)
- Which products matter most right now?

## 3. Goals for the next 30 days
- Launch, campaign, clearing stock…?
- Ad budget: is there one, and who controls it? (spending is always level 0 for agents, and locked there)
